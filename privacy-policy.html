Comfortrama Heating and Cooling Inc. — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Comfortrama Heating and Cooling Inc. ("Comfortrama," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the personal information you share with us through our website (comfortrama.com), phone calls, contact forms, and advertising (including Google Ads and Meta/Facebook and Instagram ads).

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you interact with us:

  • Contact information you provide voluntarily, such as your name, phone number, email address, and home address, when you request a quote, fill out a form on our website, or submit a lead form on Facebook or Instagram.
  • Service details you share with us, such as the type of HVAC service you're interested in (furnace, AC, water heater, gas line, etc.) and details about your home or equipment.
  • Website usage data, such as pages visited, time on site, and general location (city/region), collected automatically through tools like Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel.
  • Advertising interaction data, such as which ads you clicked or which lead form you submitted, when you engage with our Google or Meta ad campaigns.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to quote requests and schedule service calls or installations
  • To contact you by phone, text, or email about your inquiry
  • To improve our website, services, and advertising based on aggregated usage trends
  • To measure the performance of our advertising campaigns (Google Ads, Meta/Facebook Ads)
  • To comply with legal or regulatory obligations

3. Advertising & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses tracking tools, including Google Ads conversion tracking and the Meta (Facebook) Pixel, to understand which ads lead to inquiries and to measure conversions. These tools may use cookies or similar technologies to collect information about your visit. This helps us measure ad performance and does not give us access to your Facebook or Google account details beyond what you choose to share with us directly (for example, through a lead form).

If you submit an inquiry through a Facebook or Instagram Instant Form, the information you provide (such as name, phone number, and email) is shared with us by Meta so that we can follow up with you regarding your request.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate our business (e.g., form processing, scheduling, or SMS/email follow-up tools)
  • Legal authorities, if required to comply with a law, regulation, or valid legal request

5. Data Retention

We retain your contact and service information for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, provide service, and maintain business records, or as required by law.

6. Your Choices

  • You may ask us to update, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the information below.
  • You may opt out of promotional text messages or emails at any time by replying "STOP" or using the unsubscribe link provided.
  • You can manage cookie and ad-tracking preferences through your browser settings or through your Facebook/Google ad preferences.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for homeowners and businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
